# Fresh & Local redesign

Orange (#ff5a00), navy (#101f35) and white, inspired by the supplied desktop/mobile reference. Uses the real restaurant catalog and preserves Arabic/English, staff permissions, notifications and customer ordering. Favourites are stored in this browser. Categories, search and filters operate on actual catalog data.

Hero asset: public/images/qanater-hero.png. Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Illustrative marketing image inspired by the local barrages; not documentary photography.

Generation prompt: Premium wide panoramic photographic-style hero inspired by Mohamed Ali Nile barrages at Al Qanater El Khayria: sandstone arches and modest ornate towers, Nile water, palms, warm late-afternoon sunlight. Left third in leafy shadow for UI typography. Subtle Egyptian kebab and rice plate at the lower right. Natural travel and food photography aesthetic. No text, logo, UI, frame or watermark.
